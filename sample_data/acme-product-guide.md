# Acme Analytics — Product Guide

Welcome to Acme Analytics, a fictional software company. This guide describes
our three products so you can ask the assistant questions about them.

## 1. Pulse

Pulse is a real-time dashboard that streams your key business metrics to a
single screen. It connects to over 40 data sources, including databases,
spreadsheets, and third-party APIs, and refreshes every five seconds.

Pulse is best for operations teams that need to monitor live performance.
It starts at $49 per user per month.

## 2. Insight

Insight is a report builder that turns raw data into scheduled, shareable
reports. You can drag and drop charts, add written commentary, and email the
finished report to stakeholders on a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule.

Insight is best for analysts who prepare recurring reports. It starts at
$29 per user per month.

## 3. Forecast

Forecast is a forecasting tool that uses historical data to predict future
demand, revenue, and inventory. It includes seasonality detection and lets you
compare several scenarios side by side.

Forecast is best for finance and supply-chain teams that plan ahead. It starts
at $99 per user per month.

## Support and pricing

All three products include a 14-day free trial, email support, and a 99.9%
uptime service-level agreement. Annual billing saves 20% on any plan.
